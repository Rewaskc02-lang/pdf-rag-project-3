Acknowledgement Quick principle: Be respectful, protect information, communicate early, and take ownership of your work. 1. Purpose and scope This rulebook describes the everyday standards expected at Northstar Digital Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Northstar"). It applies to employees, interns, temporary staff and contractors while working on company business, at company premises, remotely, or at work-related events. These guidelines are a practical summary and do not replace an employment agreement, applicable law, or a more specific written policy. Where requirements conflict, applicable law and the signed employment agreement take precedence. 2. Professional conduct 2.1 Respect and inclusion Treat colleagues, clients, vendors and visitors with courtesy. Discrimination, bullying, harassment, intimidation, threats and retaliation are not acceptable. Differences of opinion should be handled calmly and professionally. 2.2 Integrity Be honest in records, reports, timesheets, expenses and business communications. Do not offer or accept improper payments, gifts or favours. Disclose personal interests that could affect a business decision. 2.3 Ownership Meet agreed commitments, raise risks early, document important decisions and ask for help when blocked. Give credit to contributors and do not misrepresent another person's work as your own. 3. Working hours and attendance Standard working hours are set by the relevant team and communicated by the manager. Employees should be ready to work at their scheduled start time and record attendance using the approved system. Flexible or remote work arrangements require manager approval and must preserve team coverage, productivity and information security. If you will be late or absent unexpectedly, notify your manager as soon as reasonably

possible. Overtime or work on a scheduled rest day should be approved in advance and handled in accordance with applicable law and company procedures. 4. Leave and time off Submit planned leave through the designated HR system with reasonable notice. Emergency or sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as practical, followed by any required documentation under the applicable policy. Leave balances, eligibility, holidays and carry-forward rules are governed by the employee's contract and the current HR leave policy. Managers should assess requests consistently and communicate decisions promptly. Do not record leave or attendance inaccurately, or ask another person to do so on your behalf. 5. Communication and meetings Use company-approved channels for work discussions. Keep messages clear, professional and relevant; avoid sharing confidential information in public channels or with unauthorised recipients. Meetings should have a purpose and, where useful, an agenda. Join on time, participate constructively and record decisions, owners and deadlines. Respect colleagues' focus time and working hours. External statements, press comments and public announcements on behalf of Northstar may be made only by authorised representatives. 6. Information security and privacy Protect passwords, authentication codes, devices, customer information, source code, business plans and employee data. Use strong unique passwords and multi-factor authentication wherever available. Store company information only in approved systems. Do not forward work data to personal email, upload it to unapproved services, or use public AI tools with confidential or personal information unless expressly authorised. Report suspected phishing, lost devices, accidental disclosures, unauthorised access or other security incidents immediately to the IT/security team. Do not investigate or conceal an incident yourself. Collect, access and use personal information only for legitimate work purposes and only to the extent needed. Follow applicable privacy and data-protection requirements. 7. Acceptable use of company assets Company laptops, software, accounts, networks, phones and subscriptions are provided primarily for business use. Limited incidental personal use may be allowed if it is lawful, safe, does not interfere with work and does not create material cost or risk. Do not install unlicensed software, disable security controls, bypass access restrictions, use company systems for unlawful activity, or connect unknown storage devices without approval. Keep equipment secure and in good condition. Return all company property, access cards, documents and credentials when requested or when your engagement ends. 8. Workplace health, safety and respect Follow site safety instructions, emergency procedures and building rules. Keep work areas clear and report hazards, injuries, near misses or unsafe conditions promptly.

Alcohol, illegal drugs, weapons, threats and violence are prohibited at work or during company activities, subject to applicable law and any specifically authorised event arrangements. Maintain professional boundaries in person and online. The company prohibits harassment and retaliation for raising a concern in good faith. 9. Performance and learning Managers and employees should agree on clear goals, priorities and measures of success. Feedback should be timely, specific and respectful. Employees are encouraged to pursue relevant training and share knowledge. Use company learning resources responsibly and obtain approval before committing the company to paid courses, certifications or events. Performance concerns should be discussed directly, with reasonable expectations, support and opportunities to improve. 10. Expenses and travel Obtain approval before booking business travel or incurring non-routine expenses. Choose reasonable, cost-effective options consistent with the travel and expense policy. Submit accurate claims with itemised receipts and a clear business purpose within the stated deadline. Personal expenses, duplicate claims and altered receipts are not reimbursable. Travel must comply with safety guidance, client requirements and applicable laws. 11. Reporting concerns and discipline Raise concerns about misconduct, safety, discrimination, fraud, conflicts of interest or security incidents with your manager, HR or the designated reporting channel. You may bypass your manager if they are involved or if doing so feels unsafe. Reports will be handled as sensitively as possible and reviewed fairly. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is prohibited. Violations may result in coaching, retraining, formal warnings, access restrictions or termination of employment/engagement, depending on the facts, seriousness, applicable law and company procedures. The employee will have an appropriate opportunity to respond. 12. Acknowledgement I acknowledge that I have received and read this sample rulebook. I understand that I am responsible for following the policies relevant to my role and asking HR or my manager if I need clarification.
